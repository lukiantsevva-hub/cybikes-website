HP116E Electric Pit Bike

The HIGHPER HP116E is a configurable electric pit bike offered with 12/10 or 14/12 wheel setups. The 12/10 base configuration uses a 1,000 W, 36 V brushless motor and a 36 V · 10.4 Ah lithium battery. The 14/12 configuration starts with a 1,600 W, 48 V motor and 48 V · 15.6 Ah battery package.

€1,350

Base retail price €1,350 for 12/10 wheels. The 14/12 wheel setup carries a €100 upgrade.

HP117E Electric Pit Bike

The HIGHPER HP117E is a full-size electric pit bike with a 5,000 W, 72 V QS brushless motor and a 72 V · 30 Ah lithium battery. It is offered with 17/14 or 19/16 wheel configurations. The larger 19/16 setup has a higher stated seat height and maximum speed; performance figures should be treated as factory specifications and confirmed for the ordered configuration.

€2,600

Base retail price €2,600 with 17/14 wheels. The 19/16 wheel setup is a €100 upgrade.
